# Level 1 palette and camera pass

- **Goal:** Bring the first playable level closer to the selected bright, graphic turquoise, navy and orange palette, and widen the third-person tactical view.
- **Palette:** Applied the existing original palette to all eight blockout materials (`Concrete`, `OutpostMasonry`, `Asphalt`, `Pavement`, `Interior`, `Cover`, `Window`, and `RustedMetal`) and saved/recompiled them in Unreal Editor.
- **Camera:** Increased the spring-arm distance from 650 cm to 1,200 cm and lowered the initial pitch from -48 to -58 degrees. Because the player Blueprint retained its older inherited default, the new distance and no-collision tactical arm are also enforced on spawn. This should expose more of the street lanes while keeping the player directly controllable.
- **Runtime check:** Unreal MCP connected to `/Game/Maps/LVL_01_MorrowDistrict_Walled`; PIE started successfully. The first wave spawned six zombies across the west, east and south lanes. They moved to within about 203 cm of their wall targets, inside the current 255 cm attack range. The code's attack timer should therefore reach the wall, but outpost integrity loss has not yet been read back directly.
- **Open issues:** A captured PIE frame still showed a washed-out/over-bright view and the bullet widget read `0 | 0`. The imported zombie is still in its reference pose because its mesh has no matching animation set. Hotkeys 2–7 were not validated; Slate key injection reached the editor but did not demonstrate an in-game purchase.
- **Next:** Build and launch the updated camera, inspect lighting and palette in PIE, measure outpost health during attacks, validate purchase/repair/ammo hotkeys with a reliable viewport-focused input method, and fix the bullet count display. Retarget or replace the zombie idle, walk and attack animations.
